## Recording policy

Keep the three explicitly named live hits in the curated source: Gosta de Rua
(Ao Vivo), P do Pecado (Ao Vivo), Tubarões (Ao Vivo). They request that recording
in the source itself. Do not strip their live marker to increase coverage. Other
live versions remain misses unless an individual source entry explicitly requests
them. Matching an artist/title to Deezer can still select a later studio
re-recording with an untagged title; this requires listening or recording-level
identifiers to fully verify.

## Remaining verification work

Every curated row not covered above still needs independent performer/year
verification. In particular, Chega de Saudade mixes Elizeth Cardoso and João
Gilberto interpretations; Águas de Março uses the 1974 duet rather than earlier
recordings; Sozinho uses Caetano's interpretation rather than the composition's
first recording. These are performer-specific years, not the first release of
any interpretation. Touradas em Madri's year remains unverified here.

The final build report lists additional problems found in the scraped data,
excluded rows with no performer, and completion counts for each pipeline stage.

## Scraped-category corrections

- Portuguese language alone is no longer treated as evidence of Brazilian
  origin. This removes Portuguese songs such as **A Minha Casinha** (Milú /
  Xutos e Pontapés) from the category input. Chart entries are retained even when
  the artist is international: they really appeared on the Brazilian charts.
- **No Rancho Fundo / Chitãozinho & Xororó**: use the named duo's **1989**
  interpretation, not the song category's 1931. [Article and version discography](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Rancho_Fundo).
- **Brasileirinho / Waldir Azevedo**: release **1949**, not the 1947 composition /
  recording year. [Article infobox](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brasileirinho).
- **Aquarela do Brasil**: replace the infobox's composer credit with Francisco
  Alves; the IMS/BN evidence above identifies the 1939 recording.
- **O Que É que a Baiana Tem?**: use the verified joint Carmen Miranda / Dorival
  Caymmi credit for the 1939 recording, avoiding two entries for the same take.
- Curated release years take precedence over conflicting inferred category
  years, so the verified **Jura / Mário Reis / 1928** correction survives the
  category's 1929 date.
