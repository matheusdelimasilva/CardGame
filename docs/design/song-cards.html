Deck back

The Vinyl card face down: no year, title or artist, just a gold label with the spindle hole and "???". The label is always gold so its color can't give the decade away. It has two states. Static: the record sits still and nothing plays. Playing: the record spins, bars bounce and the song plays. With sound on, hover the static card to switch it to playing.
